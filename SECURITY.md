# Security reporting

Do not publish credentials, customer configurations, reports, addresses, or
exploitable vulnerability details in a public issue.

Use GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** option when it is available, or contact
your established NetConverter support contact privately to arrange secure
submission. If you have no private contact, open a public issue asking for a
private reporting channel without including the sensitive details.

For a suspected credential exposure, revoke or rotate the affected credential
with its issuer. Deleting a local file or Keychain item does not revoke the key.
See [the privacy boundary](docs/PRIVACY.md) and
[current verification limits](docs/VERIFICATION.md).
